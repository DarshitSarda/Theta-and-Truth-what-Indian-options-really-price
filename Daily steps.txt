Dates are automatic: every step labels data with the latest finished NSE session (IST clock + NSE holiday list),
not the computer's date. On a weekend/holiday, 02 says "nothing to do" and scrapes nothing. During market hours
(09:00-15:30 IST) 02 refuses to run. Best time: after 21:00 IST (~11:30 AM New York) when OI is final.
To reprocess an older session in 04/06, set SESSION_OVERRIDE = "YYYY-MM-DD" in their first cell.

Paper trading: never skip a monthly expiry (settlement) day - that is the day the paper engine opens the next
month's position. Next ones: 27-Oct-2026, then the November monthly (23-Nov-2026 as currently listed).
